




# natural-resources-and-growth
a short, independent economics project exploring the feasibility of the "Curse of Natural Resources" in the 31 years since the original 1995 Sachs and Warner paper, with empirical analysis using R. 



**what is it** 
The Curse of Natural Resources is a theory that countries with large resource endowments have impacted economic growth as a result of such endowments. One landmark paper which I will be basing my analysis off of is "The Curse of Natural Resources", a 1995 working paper by Sachs and Warner later published as a full paper in the EER in 2001. The paper outlines the curses feasibility given the contemporary data at the time, along with why the curse may exist. The paper determined that, the curse likely did have grounds in economic theory based mainly on... reasons of dutch disease and crowding out.... 

**my research question**
i wish to know if the curse has backing with an extra 31 years of data. 

**what am i testing**
im approximating the sxp variable(S&Ws measure of resource intensity) with easily available data and both visualisng their patterns with growht rates and also regressing with growth rates, so we can see if these two things are related anymore. 

**brief headline findings**
my findings provide some evidence to the curse's existence, but perhaps the results aren't as convincing as they were when the paper was first published.
